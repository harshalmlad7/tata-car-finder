How recommendations are selected

1 · Your budgetWe first look for cars within the amount you selected.
2 · People travellingThe car must be able to carry the number of people you selected.
3 · Where you driveWe compare the cars for city, city + highway, or highway use.
4 · Daily drivingWe compare which fuel choices are practical for how much you drive every day.
5 · Home chargingIf an EV is considered, we check how easy regular charging would be.

Budget and passenger capacity come first. Cars above your selected budget or without enough seats are removed. Driving, daily distance and charging then help us compare the cars that remain.
